What the spread says

The three bases separate cleanly by pitch: Sophion 77.7 Hz, Destiny 92.3 Hz, Kate 193.5 Hz — the instructions landed. Within a voice, emotion moves the same way a person does: Sophion angry rises 77.7 → 104.4 Hz, Destiny grieving drops 92.3 → 83.6 Hz and flattens (spread 24 Hz against 52 on the angry line). That is the descriptions doing work, not a filter.
