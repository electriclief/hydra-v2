WakeWord

door on port 8164

Watches for the phrase 'are you listening' with the Windows speech grammar - instant, no model, no download - and POSTs a hit to the hub, which owns the expensive listening. Reports sleeping, armed or failed with the reason, and can test its matcher against a wav file.

Concepts

Why not a model

whisper over 2 s chunks is ~2x slower than realtime on this laptop, so a model-based spotter never catches up. A one-phrase grammar is instant and needs no weights.

Three states

sleeping, armed, failed - and failed carries the reason (no_mic, no_speech_engine, child_exit). It never says armed while nothing is listening.

The trigger records nothing

It posts {phrase, text, confidence} to the hub, which owns the capture. That separation is what lets the trigger live on a weak box.

    Wiring
  • needs: powershell + System.Speech, CommsNode :8160
  • gives: POST /api/arm, POST /api/disarm, GET /api/status, POST /api/test {wav}
