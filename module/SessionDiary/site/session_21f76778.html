- **Qwen3.5-4B-3D-Coder** — Trending Models (ccore, GGUF-only release) — A 4B Qwen3.5 fine-tune specialized in three.js/3D scene code, small enough to keep resident on the 1660 while you iterate on graphics work.
- **"Channel Selection for Mixture-of-Experts Decoding Needs No Calibration Data"** — Daily Papers (18 upvotes) — Targets exactly the consumer-GPU pain point of loading routed-expert weights from VRAM at batch size 1 and shows channel selection needs no calibration set, which is the kind of trick that makes MoE quants viable inside 16GB.
- **LiquidAI LFM3-1.7B / LFM3-VL-2B** — Trending Models — Liquid's 1.7B edge text model plus a 2B any-to-any vision variant, both small enough to run fully on the 1660 for always-on tooling.

*Skipped as not hardware-relevant:* RecursiveSI/flint (39.8B), Nanbeige4.3-33B, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash GGUF quants (top GGUF downloads but far past 8GB), and the ROCmFP4 "Strix" GGUF family — AMD-optimized but built for Ryzen AI Max / RDNA3.5+ and requiring a custom runtime that won't load in stock llama.cpp.
