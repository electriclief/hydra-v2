- **NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB** — *HF Trending Models (nvidia, ~64k downloads, 275 likes; companion unsloth GGUF at 162k)* — Official NVIDIA "low-memory, low-TTFT" SLM (8B total / 4B effective, Q4_K_M single-file GGUF, 64K context, llama.cpp target), i.e. built to run well inside your 6GB 1660's budget.
- **LiquidAI LFM3-1.7B (+ official GGUF)** — *HF Trending Models (#3 by trend, 528 likes day one)* — A 1.7B edge-oriented model with a first-party GGUF already published, so it comfortably occupies a fraction of either GPU's VRAM and is the safest quality-per-byte pick of the cycle.
- **XHToken Spark-X2.6-4B (+ GGUF, 221k downloads)** — *HF Trending Models (915 likes, Apache-2.0)* — Explicitly positioned as an "on-device agent" 4B with community GGUF quants, a clean Q4 fit on the 1660 and fast headroom on the 6800.
- **abenzerps/Qwen-Image-2.1-Uncensored-Quantized** — *HF Trending Models (5.3k likes, 2.5M downloads, comfyui-gguf + MLX + FP8/NVFP4)* — Community GGUF/MLX quant pack for Qwen-Image-2.1 in ComfyUI format, the practical route to running that generator on the 16GB RX 6800 (skip on the 1660; content filter removed, so treat outputs as unfiltered).

**Papers**

- **Intrinsic Distribution Matching** — *HF Daily Papers, inclusionAI (233 upvotes, top of cycle)* — One-/few-step text-to-image distillation that drops extra score networks, so it directly cuts denoising steps — the main runtime/VRAM cost when sampling images on a 1660 or 6800.
- **Look Only When Needed: Certificate-Guided Visual Routing for Efficient Diffusion VLMs (LOWN)** — *HF Daily Papers, KAIST AI (84 upvotes)* — Shows the image only matters at ~22.8% of diffusion-VLM decoding steps on average and routes around it otherwise, a large inference-cost saving for diffusion LLM/VLM work on consumer cards.
- **EmbeddingGemma 2** — *HF Daily Papers, Google (27 upvotes)* — Unified multimodal embedding model that ships quantized weights, selectable encoders and truncatable dimensions, making a genuinely useful retrieval stack feasible on <=8GB.
- **AB-Attention** — *HF Daily Papers (3 upvotes — low signal, high relevance)* — Training-free block-sparse attention for video diffusion transformers, the kind of change that makes short video generations tractable on the RX 6800.

**Notes:** No paper or model in this cycle advertised AMD/ROCm-specific optimization; the hardware fit above is inferred from published quant formats, parameter counts and stated footprints, not vendor benchmarks. On ROCm, the LFM3 and Spark GGUF builds are the ones worth testing first (llama.cpp), while the Nemotron 4GB file is the only one NVIDIA itself documents at that footprint.
