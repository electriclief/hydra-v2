**Notable for your RX 6800 / GTX 1660 setup**

- **Embeddings Are Overpriced? A Frozen 16 MB Vocabulary for Small and Ternary Language Models** — *HF Daily Papers (#2610.22428)* — Replaces the embedding table with a frozen 16 MB projection so a ternary (BitNet b1.58) Gemma-shaped model ships as a **46 MB file** and runs at 615–740 tok/s in 62 MB of RAM on a laptop CPU, 5–6× faster than the float model's 8-bit GGUF — the single best hardware-fit item this cycle if you want a tiny local text model or the 1660's CPU fallback path.
- **Ternary-Bonsai-2-9B-gguf** — *prism-ml, HF Trending (103 likes / 77.8k downloads, updated Oct 15)* — A 9B *vision-language* model at **2.42 GB** (PQ2_0) or 2.00 GB (PTQ1_0) with 262K context and custom ternary llama.cpp kernels (CUDA/Metal/CPU) — the whole thing fits in the GTX 1660's 6 GB with room for image context, which almost nothing at this capability level does.
- **MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B + its GGUF repo** — *OpenBMB, HF Trending (263 trending / 89 likes; GGUF repo touched today)* — Official 1B on-device VLM: Q4_K_M is **0.67 GB** plus a 0.90 GB mmproj, with image, multi-image and video understanding — a drop-in OCR/vision worker for either card at negligible VRAM; the 6 GB 1660 can run it alongside another model.
- **humanizer (12B, Gemma-4 finetune)** — *jialinyyzz, HF Trending #6 today (1,979 likes / 110k downloads)* — Genuinely viral local rewrite model with QAT quants sized for your hardware: **IQ2_XS-QAT 3.89 GB** (fits the 1660) and **Q4_K_M 7.63 GB** (comfortable on the 6800's 16 GB) — the strongest quality-per-GB text model in today's trending list.
- **LFM2.5-24B-A2B-GGUF** — *Liquid AI, Oct 9 (214 likes / 120k downloads)* — Edge-targeted hybrid MoE (24B total, **2B active**); only the QAD-Q4_0 at **13.58 GB** clears the 6800's 16 GB, so it's an AMD-card-only option and the 1660 is out — noted because 2B-active inference makes it unusually fast per token for its quality tier.
- **llama.cpp b11741 (Oct 19)** — *ggml-org releases* — Adds OpenCL cooperative-k dp4a GEMM for **q4_0/q4_k/q6_k**, i.e. faster quantized matmul on the OpenCL backend used by AMD cards — minor/incremental, but it's the only AMD-specific optimization that landed today.

**Not a fit, for the record:** the day's biggest release, *DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017* (trending #1 on Models), is a 510 GB checkpoint — even its 2-bit ROCm repack is 383 GB. Skip it; no consumer-GPU path exists.
