## What changed in the rig to be data-driven

- `characters.py` applies `body_scale` **once** and emits a `derived` block (`head_r/shoulder_w/torso_h/upper_arm/forearm/neck_w/hip_w/chest_w/hair_drop/hair_wave`); `rig.py` reads only that. Height and limb length stay consistent instead of a stretched bitmap.
- Scene owns staging (`anchor`, `focus`, `framing`, `bias`, `foreground`); overlay is drawn in **screen space after the camera crop** — that was the bug that made lower-thirds get cropped away at tighter framings.
- Mouth height comes from the RMS envelope in `rig.envelope` and is capped at `0.155R`; `expression.state` adds it to whatever preset is playing instead of replacing it.
- Gestures are three keyframe tables (`calm/animated/loose`); blink is `lid_open*0.95` from a per-character schedule (`blink_speed` 0.8/1.0/1.35).
- Head roll rotates a cropped head layer (hair, rig, face) about the neck; expression params move feature positions with yaw/pitch parallax.
- Static lighting blurs are cached — chronicler frame time went 2.1 s → 0.37 s.
