- **LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B** | HF Trending (#2 by trending score: 594, 1.5k likes) | Official Liquid AI edge release that ships with **AMD-specific benchmarks** (Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Radeon 8060S, llama.cpp Vulkan) — ~1 GB at 4-bit, Q4_0 GGUF, 2.5× faster prefill and 1.8× decode than comparators at 32K context, positioned as replacing 9B-class agent workloads. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B

- **Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo-Quantized** | HF Trending (273 score, 5.6k likes, 306k downloads) | GGUF/ComfyUI-GGUF port of the 7B, 8-step Turbo image model with a **Q4_K_M at 4.05 GB** (Q4_0 same size) — the only text-to-image entry this cycle that lands inside 8 GB with the text encoder loaded. https://huggingface.co/abenzerps/Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo-Quantized

- **Qwen3.5-4B-3D-Coder (GGUF + vision)** | HF Trending (#7: 363 score, 123k downloads) | Independent CCORE preview fine-tune of Qwen3.5-4B for Three.js/3D scene code, with **Q4_K_M at 2.71 GB plus a 644 MB mmproj** so you can feed a reference image and get a scene back — fully VRAM-safe next to a browser on either card. https://huggingface.co/ccore/Qwen3.5-4B-3D-Coder

- **LoCo — "Think Less, Locally: Local Preference Learning for Reasoning Compression"** | HF Daily Papers (most-upvoted today: 64 upvotes) | DPO on state-conditioned preference pairs cuts reasoning completion length **41.7–45.4% on math and 23.4–36.3% on code with no accuracy loss** — the single biggest decode-time lever for a 1660, where token count dominates wall-clock cost. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04814

**Also flagged, lower priority:** `LiquidAI/LFM3-Omni-2B` (any-to-any: audio + vision + speech-to-speech, 4.33 GB bf16 → ~2.2 GB at 4-bit, 351 likes) is the cheapest local omni-modal option seen; `sensenova/SenseNova-Loop-9B-Preview` is a second looped-architecture release but needs ~5–6 GB at Q4 and has thin traction (2.7k downloads).
